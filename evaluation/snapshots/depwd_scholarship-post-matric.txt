🎓 Who can apply?
Students in Class 11 or 12.
Students in Diploma, Degree or Post-Graduate courses.
Indian nationals only.
Students with a benchmark disability of 40% or more.
🪪 Must have
UDID Card.
Aadhaar Card.
Family income up to Rs. 2.5 lakh per year.
The student should not receive another post-scholarship or stipend.
💰 Money support
Tuition/admission fee: up to Rs. 1.4 lakh.
Other fee: up to Rs. 10,000.
Disability allowance: Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 4,000 per year.
Book allowance: Rs. 1,500 per year.
🏠 Monthly support
Hosteller: Rs. 1,200 per month.
Day scholar: Rs. 650 per month.
Given for 12 months.
✅ Important rules
Maximum 2 children with disabilities from the same parents can receive it.
post-scholarship is for one class or one academic year.
Check the latest rules before applying.

Apply online on the
National post-scholarship Portal
